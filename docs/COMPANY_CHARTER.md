# Company Charter — Draft

## Identity
A legally and operationally independent technology company focused on software engineering, business automation and applied AI.

## Ownership and relationship
The company is separate from Royal Egypt Group. Any cooperation is strategic and contractual, with distinct management, accounts, infrastructure, intellectual property, customer records and data processing.

## Operating principles
1. Free, open-source and local-first where practical; no new recurring cost without explicit owner approval.
2. Customer value and collected revenue before speculative platform expansion.
3. Least privilege, tenant isolation, auditable actions and recoverable operations.
4. AI agents may execute only within explicit scopes and resource limits.
5. Human approval is mandatory for contracts, payments, transfers, borrowing, legal commitments, ownership changes and sensitive permission changes.
6. No fabricated claims, capabilities, customers, revenue, certifications or testimonials.
7. Every production change must be tested, reviewed, reversible and documented.

## Brand status
No company name, logo, domain, legal entity or jurisdiction has been approved. Do not publish the placeholder identity as a real registered business.
