# Revenue Plan — Initial Hypothesis

## First revenue: productized implementation services
Offer clearly scoped projects such as:
- Customer enquiry intake and routing
- Appointment / follow-up automation
- Internal operations dashboards
- Website and database integrations
- AI-assisted support with human handoff

Commercial model: fixed setup fee plus optional monthly maintenance. Final prices must follow customer discovery, delivery-cost estimates and local legal/tax review.

## Recurring revenue: productize repeated work
Track repeated customer problems and convert the strongest validated workflow into a small SaaS product.

## Rules
- No guaranteed revenue claims.
- Do not build a large platform before securing evidence of willingness to pay.
- Track collected revenue, gross margin, support load, churn and delivery hours.
- Royal Egypt Group may be a pilot customer only under a separate scope and data boundary.
